Results are output to maddenData.txt.
- Contains list of teams in order of first appearance. Each team listing includes team legacy, wins, loses, and player legacy, mvps, and dpotgs.
- Teams are then ranked in order of legacy scores
- Players are ranked in order of legacy scores
- Average team and player legacies are reported
- Dynasties are listed in order of first appearance. A dynasty is a string of 3 or more appearances with a difference of no more than 7 championships from each appearance to the next
